About

Hello, I'm Ashiqur.

Most people online know me as ashiqur. My full name is Ashiqur Rahman, and this is my personal corner of the internet — a place to think slowly, in public, without a feed pushing back.

I'm interested in the space between doing and overdoing: how much of what we call "productive" is really just noise dressed up as effort. Most of what I write here starts as a note to myself and ends up being for whoever needs to hear it too.

I keep the site itself the way I try to keep my days — a little quiet, a little deliberate, nothing on the page that isn't earning its place.

Now
  • Reading One long book at a time, slower than I'd like to admit.
  • Writing A short essay every few weeks — no schedule, no pressure.
  • Working on This site, mostly. Small edits, most days.
  • Based in Wherever the next paragraph gets written.
Colophon

This site is set in three typefaces used on purpose rather than by default: Newsreader for headings and titles, Work Sans for the paragraphs you're reading now, and IBM Plex Mono for dates, tags, and the small print. No frameworks, no tracking, no cookie banner — just HTML and CSS, built to load fast and stay out of your way.
